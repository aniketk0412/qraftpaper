## 6. Objection handling — "why pay when ChatGPT is free?"

This is THE conversion blocker. Handle it directly, in order of preference:

1. **Let them feel the moment.** Best fix is a "try one generation" so they see
   a matched paper before paying — no amount of copy beats that "whoa, this
   looks like our paper" reaction.
2. **Anchor the price against the real alternative**, not against $0: a printed
   coaching mock, a guide book, a tutor hour. "[PRICE] — less than one printout."
3. **Group pricing / sharing** turns the price wall into a sharing mechanic:
   4 friends split it, each pays less, and quizzes are shareable anyway.
4. **Reframe the value:** ChatGPT sells you *questions* (a commodity).
   QraftPaper sells you *walking in knowing what the paper looks like* (the
   outcome). You're not paying for AI; you're paying for not getting blindsided.
